How I invest

My investment approach

Investment Thesis

My investment thesis focuses on analyzing companies with strong fundamentals, and recognizing competitive advantages like high margins and monopolies. I also apply technical analysis to figure out stock bottoms or entry points for swing trades.

Investment Strategy

My strategy combines fundamental analysis and strict risk management. I utilize a mix of long-term stock or option holdings, and hedge with covered calls or VIX if warranted. Capital allocation is structured to balance growth opportunities with downside protection, ensuring sustainable portfolio performance.
